National Socialist Party Ideology Nazi ideology, as developed by the National Socialist German Workers’ Party under Adolf Hitler, was built around a set of interconnected beliefs that presented themselves as a worldview explaining Germany’s problems and offering “solutions.” At the core was a racial hierarchy. Nazis placed so-called “Aryans” at the top and persecuted Jews and other groups. Lebensraum (“living space”) justified expansion eastward. Democracy and communism were rejected in favour of authoritarian rule under the Führer. The ideology led to dictatorship, war, and genocide.
