National Socialist Party Ideology Nazi ideology, as developed by the National Socialist German Workers’ Party under Adolf Hitler, was built around a worldview explaining Germany’s politics, race, and society. It promoted racial hierarchy, authoritarian rule, and territorial expansion. It rejected democracy and communism. This ideology contributed to dictatorship, war, and genocide.
